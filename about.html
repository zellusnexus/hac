About Us · Our Story

Craftsmanship forged through legacy.

Since 1958, Huntsville American Cabinets has built custom cabinetry for the homes and businesses of the Tennessee Valley.

Since 1958

A Huntsville original.

Huntsville American Cabinets opened its doors in 1958, as Huntsville was growing from a quiet cotton town into the Rocket City. The families and businesses moving into the region needed homes and workplaces — and those spaces needed quality cabinetry.

More than 60 years later, we’re still here, still building in Huntsville, and still committed to the same simple idea: make every piece fit its space, build it to last, and deliver it when we said we would.

That commitment has made us the largest custom cabinet manufacturer in the Tennessee Valley, and an establishment in the community we’re proud to call home.

Our History

Six decades of growth

  1. 1958

    Founded in Huntsville

    The shop opens, building cabinetry for a fast-growing city.

  2. 1970s–80s

    Growing with the region

    As Huntsville expands, so does our customer base — from new neighborhoods to new businesses.

  3. 1990s–2000s

    Commercial expansion

    We grow our commercial casework and millwork capabilities and earn AWI certification.

  4. Today

    The Tennessee Valley’s largest

    The largest custom cabinet manufacturer in the region, serving homeowners, builders, architects, and businesses.

Our People

Our team includes designers, engineers, cabinetmakers, finishers, and installers — many with decades in the trade. Their experience, passed from one generation of craftspeople to the next, is the real secret behind our process.
