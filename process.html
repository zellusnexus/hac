Our Process

Perfected over more than 60 years.

From the first conversation to the final walkthrough, here’s what to expect when you build with us.

  1. 01

    Consult

    Every project begins with a conversation. We review your plans or ideas, visit and measure the space, and learn how it will be used, your style preferences, and your budget and timeline.

    • Initial meeting at your site or our shop
    • Review of plans, inspiration, and requirements
    • Field measurements
    • Budget and schedule discussion
  2. 02

    Design

    Our designers turn what we heard into drawings and selections. You’ll see layouts, elevations, and finish samples, and we’ll refine them together until everything is right.

    • Layouts, elevations, and shop drawings
    • Wood species, door style, and finish samples
    • Hardware and storage options
    • Final approval before production
  3. 03

    Build

    Once the design is approved, your cabinetry is built in our Huntsville shop using a process we’ve perfected over 60 years — precision machining, skilled hand assembly, and careful finishing.

    • Materials selected for stability and durability
    • Precision machining and hand assembly
    • Multi-step finishing in a controlled environment
    • Quality inspection at every stage
  4. 04

    Install

    Our own installation crews deliver and install your cabinetry on schedule, protecting your home or job site and leaving everything clean and ready for countertops or the next trade.

    • Scheduled delivery that matches your timeline
    • Professional installation by our team
    • Final walkthrough and adjustments
    • Ongoing support after the job is complete

Timeline

How long does it take?

Every project is different, but here’s a general guide. We’ll give you a specific schedule during your consultation.

Consult & Design

Typically one to three weeks, depending on how quickly selections are made and the complexity of the project.

Build

Typically four to eight weeks in our shop after design approval, depending on size and finish.

Install

Often just a few days for a kitchen; larger homes and commercial projects are scheduled with your GC.

Process FAQ

Common questions

Do I need finished plans before contacting you?

No. Some clients come with full architectural drawings, others with a few photos and a tape measure. We can start from wherever you are.

Can I visit the shop?

Yes — contact us to schedule a time. Seeing the craftsmanship in person is the best way to understand the difference.

Do you handle countertops?

Our focus is cabinetry, but we coordinate closely with countertop fabricators and other trades so the handoff is seamless.

What happens if something needs adjusting after install?

We stand behind our work. Just call us and we’ll come out and make it right.
