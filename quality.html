About Us · Quality

A priority for excellence.

Our reputation for the highest standard of quality is what made us the Tennessee Valley’s largest custom cabinet manufacturer. Here’s what stands behind it.

Certifications & Memberships

Independently verified quality

Architectural Woodwork Institute

AWI’s Quality Certification Program verifies that a shop has the skills, equipment, and processes to build to the Architectural Woodwork Standards. It gives owners and architects confidence that what’s specified is what gets built.

Better Business Bureau

We’re a BBB Accredited Business with an A+ rating, committed to honest advertising, transparent business practices, and responsive customer service.

How We Build

Quality you can see. And the quality you can’t.

The difference in a well-built cabinet shows up years later — in doors that still hang straight, drawers that still glide, and finishes that still look new. We focus on what happens inside the box just as much as the surface.

  • Premium hardwoods and furniture-grade plywood selected for stability
  • Precision machining for tight, consistent joinery
  • Durable, multi-step finishing applied in a controlled environment
  • Full-extension, soft-close hardware from trusted manufacturers
  • Inspection at every stage before anything leaves the shop
