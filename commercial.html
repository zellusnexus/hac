Commercial Cabinetry

Durable, compliant, and made to make a first impression.

Commercial spaces work hard. Cabinetry in a clinic, classroom, or corporate office has to look sharp on day one and still hold up after years of constant use. We build it to do both.

We work alongside general contractors, owners, and design teams to meet project specifications, coordinate with other trades, and hit the milestones on your construction schedule.

  • AWI-certified quality woodwork for spec’d projects
  • Commercial-grade hardware, laminates, and solid surfaces
  • Shop drawings and submittals prepared in-house
  • Installation crews experienced on active job sites

Commercial FAQ

Common questions

Can you work from architectural drawings and specifications?

Yes. Most of our commercial work starts from a set of drawings. We review the specs, prepare shop drawings for approval, and build to them.

Do you bid projects through general contractors?

We do. We regularly provide pricing to GCs and owners and are happy to be added to your bid list.

How far in advance should we contact you?

As early as possible. Bringing us in during design or preconstruction helps us lock in materials and production time to match your schedule.

What areas do you serve?

We’re based in Huntsville and work throughout North Alabama and the greater Tennessee Valley. Contact us about projects outside that area.

Have a commercial project coming up?

Send us your drawings or tell us about the space. We’ll follow up with next steps and a timeline.
