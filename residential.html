Custom Residential Cabinetry

The heart of your home, built by hand in Huntsville.

Homeowner FAQ

Common questions

Do you work directly with homeowners?

Absolutely. We work directly with homeowners on remodels and new homes, and we’re glad to coordinate with your builder or designer if you have one.

How long does a custom kitchen take?

Timelines vary with size and finish, but most projects take several weeks from approved design to installation. We’ll give you a clear schedule up front.

Can you match existing cabinetry?

In many cases, yes. Because we build everything ourselves, we can often match door profiles, wood species, and finishes for additions and renovations.

How is custom different from semi-custom?

Semi-custom cabinets come in fixed sizes with limited options. Ours are built to your exact dimensions with no filler strips, and with complete freedom in style and storage.
