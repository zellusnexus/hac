Who We Serve

One shop. Four kinds of partners. The same standard for all of them.

For more than 60 years we’ve built cabinetry for the people who shape the Tennessee Valley — from a single family kitchen to a multi-floor commercial build-out.

Every project that leaves our shop is designed for its space, built to endure, and delivered when we said it would be. What changes from client to client is how we work with you.

Why Clients Choose Us

The advantages of a local manufacturer

Built in Huntsville

Our cabinetry is made in our own shop, not drop-shipped from a factory overseas. That means tighter control over quality and faster answers when you need them.

Truly custom

No stock boxes or fixed sizes. Each piece is designed and built to fit its intended space and your exact specifications.

Certified quality

As an Architectural Woodwork Institute certified shop, our work meets nationally recognized standards for materials, construction, and finish.

On time, every time

We plan production around your schedule and deliver and install in a professional manner — a reputation we’ve built since 1958.

One team, start to finish

Design, engineering, fabrication, finishing, and installation all happen under one roof with one point of contact.

Experience at scale

As the largest custom cabinet manufacturer in the Tennessee Valley, we can handle projects of nearly any size.

Not sure where your project fits?

Tell us what you’re planning. We’ll point you to the right people on our team and get back to you within one business day.
