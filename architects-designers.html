Who We Serve · Architects & Designers

Your design intent, built exactly as drawn.

We partner with architects and interior designers to turn detailed drawings into finished millwork — with the craftsmanship and documentation your projects demand.

A Trusted Fabrication Partner

We protect the details that make your design yours.

Reveals, grain matching, integrated lighting, unusual materials — the details that set a space apart are often the hardest to build. Our shop has spent decades solving exactly those problems.

We engage early, ask the right questions, and document everything so there are no surprises in the field. You get a fabricator who respects the drawings and communicates when something needs your input.

What We Provide

Support from concept to install

Design Assistance

Early input on constructability, materials, and budget so your concept is buildable before it goes to bid.

Shop Drawings

Detailed drawings prepared in-house for your review and approval before anything is built.

Samples & Mockups

Finish samples, material selections, and mockups of critical details on request.

AWI Standards

Work built to Architectural Woodwork Standards, with the certification to back it up on spec’d projects.

Specialty Materials

Veneers, solid surfaces, laminates, metal and glass integration, and custom finishes.

Field Coordination

Our installers coordinate with the GC and other trades to deliver the finished look you designed.

Specify With Confidence

Built to standard. Backed by experience.

As an Architectural Woodwork Institute certified shop, we meet published, nationally recognized standards for grade, construction, and finish — giving owners and design teams an objective measure of quality.

  • Custom, Premium, and Economy grade work per AWS
  • Residential and commercial project experience
  • Responsive project management through closeout
